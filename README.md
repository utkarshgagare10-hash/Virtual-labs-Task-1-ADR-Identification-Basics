Case Summary
A 34-year-old male developed an itchy generalized skin rash and mild facial itching approximately 36 hours after starting amoxicillin 500 mg three times daily for acute bacterial sinusitis.

Amoxicillin was discontinued on Day 3, following which the patient's symptoms significantly improved.

ADR Identification
- Suspected ADR: Cutaneous drug reaction – rash/pruritus
- Suspected Drug: Amoxicillin
- Temporal Relationship: Present
- Dechallenge: Positive
- Alternative Cause: No obvious alternative identified
- Outcome: Patient recovered

Conclusion
The case is suggestive of a drug-related cutaneous reaction associated with amoxicillin based on the temporal relationship, positive dechallenge, and known association of amoxicillin with skin reactions.
